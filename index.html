When does risk-managed momentum actually help?

An interactive backtest of a dual-momentum, volatility-targeted strategy across U.S. equities, international equities, bonds, and cash. Move the controls, especially the crash guard, to see where the strategy protects capital and where it pays for that protection. Every number here is computed from the same real 2012–2026 market data as the paper.

How to read this

This strategy is defensive, so it is not expected to beat VOO during a strong bull market. The point is to test when risk-managed momentum protects capital, when it fails, and whether that protection is statistically meaningful. The controls show the tradeoff between crash protection and whipsaw.

Main takeaway

Risk-managed momentum helped most in slower stress regimes, failed in fast crashes without a guard, and the daily crash guard revealed a clear tradeoff: faster protection reduces crash losses but increases whipsaw in calm markets.
